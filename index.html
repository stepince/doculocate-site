TWO WAYS TO USE YOUR DOCUMENT

Search to find the evidence.
Ask to analyze the whole document.

Two different jobs, one text box. Choose Search | Ask beside it.

SEARCH

Find answers, not just keywords.

Ctrl+F only finds the words you already know. Search finds passages by meaning and intent, then connects the answer to its source. Ask “How can I improve visibility?” and it can find the relevant passage even when those exact words never appear. Choose Hybrid (the default), Semantic, or Lexical for exact text.

Search documents by meaning →
ASK

Ask your document anything.

Ask sends your question and the whole document to the AI provider you choose, for summaries, explanations, key points, and broader analysis. It needs your own AI provider and key, and its answers are not linked to a highlighted passage, so use Search to check them.

Ask questions about a document →

DOCULOCATE — CTRL+F FOR MEANING

Ctrl+F matches words.
DocuLocate matches meaning.

Ctrl+F searches for matching characters and words. DocuLocate searches for concepts, intent, and meaning. When you do need the exact text, Lexical search works like Ctrl+F too.
