ONE DOCUMENT. THREE SIMPLE STEPS.

Upload. Search. Locate.

From “where does it say that?” to the passage you need.

01

Upload

Add a PDF, DOCX, TXT, XLSX, XLS, or CSV file. The app prepares it for search before opening the viewer.

02

Search

Describe what you need. Start with Hybrid, choose Semantic for meaning, or Lexical for keyword search.

03

Locate

Select a result to highlight its location in the viewer. Step between matches or open or download the original file.
