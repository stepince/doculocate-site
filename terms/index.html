About this website

This website introduces DocuLocate, a semantic document search tool. Its interactive examples use fictional documents and prepared results to illustrate how search by meaning differs from exact keyword matching. The examples are not live product output or a promise of search performance.

Review original source material

Search results, relevance labels, and answers help you locate information. An answer is either extracted from your document by the application or, if you use your own AI provider, written by that provider. Answers may contain errors; inspect their supporting passages. They do not establish that a document is accurate, complete, or appropriate for a particular decision. Review the original source and its surrounding context. DocuLocate does not replace legal, compliance, financial, or other professional review.

Product availability

Features and supported formats may change as the application develops. The website describes the current implementation where stated. Access to an application deployment may be subject to its own applicable terms and configuration.
